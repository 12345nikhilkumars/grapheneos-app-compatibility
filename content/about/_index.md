A community-maintained record of which apps work on GrapheneOS, why the ones that fail fail, and whether anything can be done about it.

## What this is not

It is not affiliated with the GrapheneOS project. It does not cover installation, and it does not argue that you should switch.

## How entries are made

Every app entry is a markdown file in this repository, and every change is a pull request. Reports are submitted by people who ran the app on a real device and recorded what happened.

Some seed entries were imported from public forum threads and issue trackers rather than tested by a contributor. Those carry a source link and are labelled as imported. They have not been reproduced, and they should be treated accordingly.
